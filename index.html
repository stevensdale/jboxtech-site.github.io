How a jBox fits into your office

jBox works alongside whatever systems you already use — file shares, email, cloud drives, CRMs, anything. Your team opens it in any browser and drops in their files.

1. jBox installs on your network.
We set it up inside your office, and it keeps running even if the internet goes down.
2. Your team uses it from a browser.
They visit a local address like http://jbox, choose a task (summarize, extract details, convert audio, draft content), and upload their files.
3. Results save back to your normal folders.
Your team downloads the results and saves them wherever they already keep documents. When the session ends, jBox wipes its working data.
jBox is a power tool for your office. You stay in control — it helps with the grunt work, but you make the decisions.
