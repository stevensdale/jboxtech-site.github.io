How a jBox fits into your office

You keep your existing case management, file server and email. jBox sits beside them as a power tool your team can open in any browser.

1. jBox installs on your network.
We deliver and configure the appliance inside your office network. It works even if your internet goes down.
2. Staff use it from a browser.
They go to a local address like http://jbox, choose a task (summarize, analyze, draft, timeline), and drop in files.
3. Results go back to your systems.
Staff download Word or PDF drafts and save them to your normal matter folders. When they end the session, jBox wipes the working data.
jBox is a tool for attorneys and staff. You choose the facts and the law. jBox handles reading, organizing and drafting. You keep control and judgment.
